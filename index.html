03 - About

Data-focused, decision-driven

I'm a data-focused professional passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insight. My work sits at the intersection of analysis and communication - pulling signal out of messy datasets and shaping it into something a decision-maker can actually use.

I spend most of my time in Power BI and DAX, building data models that stay fast and reports that stay honest. Underneath that, I lean on SQL and Python to get data clean before it ever reaches a dashboard.

What I care about most is the last step: clear, impactful visual storytelling that supports real business decisions, not just charts for their own sake.
